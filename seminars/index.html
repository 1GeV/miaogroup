Yu Guo ↗

Global Conformal Invariance

Time: 19:30-21:00

Location: Room B331, Hanlin Building 3

Online meeting: WPS Meeting ↗ · Meeting ID: 262 123 1807

This lecture introduces the global conformal group and its generators—translations, rotations, dilatations, and special conformal transformations—and derives the d-dimensional conformal algebra. It then explains how conformal symmetry fixes the form of two- and three-point correlation functions.
